# Source and attribution

Adapted from Figure 1 of **PosDiffNet: Positional Neural Diffusion for Point Cloud Registration in a Large Field of View with Perturbations**, Rui She, Sijie Wang, Qiyu Kang, Kai Zhao, Yang Song, Wee Peng Tay, Tianyu Geng and Xingchao Jian, AAAI 2024, pp. 231–239.

- [Publisher record and DOI](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v38i1.27775)
- [Authors' arXiv paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.03167)
- [Official implementation](https://github.com/AI-IT-AVs/PosDiffNet)

The source raster is Figure 1 on page 2 of the public arXiv PDF, rendered by Poppler with the page's longest edge set to 1600 pixels and cropped to `(108, 108, 1140, 714)`. It excludes the caption and paper text. The authors' PDF was used to obtain a legible raster reference; no PDF vector paths or text objects were imported into the scene. The reconstruction was authored against that raster with the repository's scientific components.

The three point-cloud visualizations retain the source pixels. Text, matrices, feature faces, module backgrounds, graph edges and routes were rebuilt as native PowerPoint objects. Small attention-mesh edges, corner radii and shading differ from the original. These differences are described in the scene and verification notes.
