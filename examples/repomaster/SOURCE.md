# Source and attribution

Adapted from Figure 1 of **RepoMaster: Autonomous Exploration and Understanding of GitHub Repositories for Complex Task Solving**, Huacan Wang, Ziyi Ni, Shuo Zhang, Shuo Lu, Sen Hu, Ziyang He, Chen Hu, Jiaye Lin, Yifu Guo, Yuntao Du and Pin Lyu, NeurIPS 2025.

- [NeurIPS proceedings](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/98da9cdb7e8af9192f1fe2cb38690d60-Abstract-Conference.html)
- [Authors' arXiv paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.21577)
- [Official implementation](https://github.com/wanghuacan/RepoMaster)

The input was the user-supplied 1279 × 908 PNG of Figure 1. The reference retains pixels `(0, 0, 1224, 812)`, removing the paper caption and excess right margin. The scene was authored against this bitmap. No PDF vectors, source diagrams or extracted PDF text were imported.

Changes: rebuilt text, section boundaries, tree/graph structures, arrows, workflow arcs and simple symbols as native PowerPoint objects. Retained 16 explicit crops for complex mascots, logos and small decorative icons. Replaced the handwritten heading font with Gabriola and rebuilt circular labels from separately rotated text characters. Fine hand-drawn edges and minor symbols are approximate.
